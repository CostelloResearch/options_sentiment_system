# Time-Window and AAPL Decomposition of the R3 Baseline
_Decomposes the R3 production baseline (+$6,775.14 / 357 taken trades / Sharpe 0.41) by entry-date month and by ticker.  All input rows are from `data/raw/backtest_*_demo_kelly.csv` filtered with the same `_taken_mask` logic as `scripts/r3_baseline.py`.  Generated by `scripts/decompose_time_ticker.py`._

> **Synthetic-sentiment caveat (load-bearing).**  Every row in the input logs has `is_synthetic=True` and `sentiment_source='synthetic_news'`.  The findings below characterise how the strategy interacts with the synthetic mean-reverting sentiment generator's specific path through 2024-05 to 2026-04, NOT how it will behave on real Finnhub + SEC sentiment.  Treat any time-window or AAPL-specific finding as a property of the synthetic corpus until validated against real-mode runs.

## Methodology

- **Universe:** 10 tickers (AAPL, GLD, IWM, JPM, NVDA, SPY, TLT, UNH, WMT, XOM).
- **Source:** `data/raw/backtest_{TICKER}_demo_kelly.csv` (10 files).
- **Filter:** `accepted=True` AND `kelly_reason` not in {rejected_by_kelly, bucket_full, portfolio_risk_cap, rejected_risk_reward, negative_kelly_*}.
- **Trades after filter:** 357 taken (signal=BUY CALLS for all).
- **Aggregate P&L:** $6,775.14 (matches the pinned $6,775 within drift tolerance).
- **Date range:** 2024-06-11 (first entry) -> 2026-04-24 (last exit).
- **Sharpe convention:** `mean(pnl_total) / std(pnl_total) * sqrt(252/5)`, matching `r3_baseline.headline()`.
- **Quintile assignment:** `data/raw/quintile_edges.json` (refit from the 10-ticker flat corpus).
- **Cumulative-curve ordering:** trades sorted by `exit_date` (P&L is realised at exit).

### 1.5 Honest assessment

The equity curve is **regime-dependent, not steady accumulation.**  Across 18 entry-months, 10 are net positive and 8 net negative.  The top 3 months alone produce $8,753.37 -- 129% of the +$6,775 headline -- which is greater than 100% because the remaining 15 months are net-negative in aggregate by roughly $1,978.23.  Put differently: remove 2025-07, 2026-04, 2025-09 from the corpus and the strategy is a net loser on the other 15 months.  The largest contiguous run-up (2024-06-25 -> 2025-10-29, $10,166.97) is 150% of total P&L, and the largest contiguous drawdown (-$8,165.83, 116 trades, 2025-10-29 -> 2026-04-02) is 121% of total P&L in the opposite direction -- meaning the strategy built up >$10k of paper P&L by 2025-10 and gave back >$8k of it by 2026-04, closing the period at +$6.8k mostly because of the April-2026 recovery month.  This is the synthetic-sentiment generator's random-walk path producing localised stretches of directional-move-then-mean-revert behaviour that the BUY CALLS strategy alternately exploits and gives back.  A real-mode rerun would have a structurally different path and these specific months would not transfer.

### 2.5 Days to earnings at entry

**Test is null in synthetic mode.**  All 44 AAPL taken trades have `days_to_earnings = 999` (the engine's sentinel for 'no earnings date found') and 313 of 313 universe-ex-AAPL trades are the same.  In demo mode the backtest engine does not invoke `earnings_filter.fetch_earnings_dates` -- it always passes 999, so the earnings-window suppression is effectively disabled and the 'AAPL is being entered too close to earnings' hypothesis cannot be tested against this corpus.  This question can only be answered against a real-mode run where `days_to_earnings` carries actual values.

### 2.6 Recommendation

**Mostly synthetic-path artifact, driven by a stop-loss-rate disparity that is itself a property of AAPL's real 2024-05 to 2026-04 price drift -- the only 'real' input under the synthetic sentiment.**  The smoking gun is the exit-reason table: AAPL hits `stop_loss` on **13 of 44 trades (29.5%)** for -$4,697.99 of drag, vs the rest of the universe at **6 of 313 trades (1.9%)** for -$1,717.08.  AAPL takes the stop loss roughly 15x more often than the universe average; the resulting stop-driven losers (-$4,697.99) are larger than AAPL's entire P&L drag (-$3,567.61), meaning AAPL's other 31 trades (time_stop + profit_target) are net positive.  Quintile inversion is consistent with this: AAPL's Q4 (-$2,087.92, 11 trades) is worse than Q5 (-$1,132.15, 11 trades), and both invert the universe pattern -- high-confidence BUY CALLS on AAPL during this real price window systematically met adverse moves.  Sample-size noise is not the primary driver (the loss is concentrated in 13 specific trades, not diffuse).  Earnings-window structural exposure cannot be tested in synthetic mode (`days_to_earnings = 999` for every row).  Read for a future real-data session: verify whether AAPL's stop-loss rate drops back toward the universe average once sentiment is real and the `earnings_filter` is live; if it does not, the spread's stop-loss sizing or AAPL's idiosyncratic vol both deserve a closer look.  The +$6,775 number itself does not need fixing -- it is a synthetic baseline by construction.

## Synthetic-sentiment caveat (repeated)

All conclusions in this report describe the synthetic-sentiment generator's interaction with the strategy, not real-data behaviour.  A real-mode rerun (`scripts/r3_baseline.py --real` once a meaningful live corpus exists) will produce a structurally different path; specific months, drawdown windows, and ticker-level attributions will not transfer.  The methodology in this report -- per-month binning, drawdown/runup decomposition, AAPL-vs-universe quintile and dte comparison -- does transfer.
