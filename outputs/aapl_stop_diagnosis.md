# AAPL Stop-Loss Concentration -- Phase 1 Diagnosis

_Read-only diagnostic of why AAPL hits `stop_loss` on 13 of 44 (29.5%) taken trades vs 6 of 313 (1.9%) for the rest of the universe in the R3 production baseline.  Tests hypotheses H1-H5 against on-disk evidence.  No production code modified in this phase.  Generated by `scripts/diagnose_aapl_stops.py`._

> **Synthetic-sentiment caveat (still load-bearing).**  Every row in the input logs has `is_synthetic=True`.  Sentiment was generated by `_generate_synthetic_history`; underlying prices and realised vol come from real yfinance history.  H3 and H4 test production-code properties that apply to real data too; H1, H2, H5 partly depend on the synthetic-real interaction and are noted as such where relevant.

## A. Stop-loss mechanics audit

**Trigger:** spread P&L hits a fixed fraction of the entry debit/credit, checked daily before profit-target and time-stop.

**Source of truth:**

- Constants -- [`signals/spread_builder.py:122-125`](../signals/spread_builder.py):
  ```python
  STOP_LOSS_DEBIT_FRAC  = 0.80   # debit: close if pnl <= -80% of entry debit
  STOP_LOSS_CREDIT_MULT = 2.00   # credit: close if spread value >= 2x entry credit
  PROFIT_TARGET_FRAC    = 0.50   # close at 50% of max profit
  TIME_STOP_DTE         = 21     # close when DTE falls to this
  ```
- Inner loop -- [`signals/spread_builder.py:996-1003`](../signals/spread_builder.py):
  ```python
  if direction == "debit":
      if pnl_per_share <= -STOP_LOSS_DEBIT_FRAC * entry_net:
          exit_reason = "stop_loss"
          ...
  ```

**Threshold value:** for a BULL_CALL_DEBIT spread (the only strategy R3 actually trades), the stop fires when the marked-to-BS spread value has fallen by 80% of the entry net debit per share.  Translation: if you paid $1.20 net for the spread, you are stopped out when the spread is worth $0.24 or less.

**Ticker-adaptivity:** *None.*  The 0.80 fraction is a global constant.  No `vol_scale`, `iv_rank` adjustment, `per_ticker_stop`, or any ticker-specific override exists in `spread_builder.py`, `engine.py`, or `position_sizer.py`.  Verified by grep over the production tree.  Every ticker, every regime, every confidence quintile uses the same 80% absolute-fraction stop.

**Why this matters for the diagnosis.**  A vol-blind stop treats a $1.20 debit on a low-vol ticker the same as a $1.20 debit on a high-vol ticker, but the *probability* of an 80% drawdown on the spread is much higher when the underlying's realised vol is higher.  This is the failure mode H3 names.

## F. Synthetic-path determinism (H1 test)

**Source:** [`backtest/engine.py:443-446`](../backtest/engine.py):

```python
def _generate_synthetic_history(ticker: str, days: int = 30) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Mean-reverting synthetic sentiment walk for demo runs."""
    seed = sum(ord(c) for c in ticker)
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    ...
```

The seed is `sum(ord(c) for c in ticker)`, which is a **deterministic function of the ticker symbol**.  AAPL's seed is `65+65+80+76 = 286` on every run.  Therefore the AAPL synthetic-sentiment path is identical across every demo-mode invocation; the same path is also identical for every other ticker (NVDA = 308, etc.).

**Implication for H1:** the AAPL anomaly cannot be explained as 'we got an unlucky RNG draw on AAPL this run.'  Same seed, same path, same anomaly, every run.  The synthetic generator's specific path *is* a property of how `_generate_synthetic_history` interacts with `ticker = 'AAPL'`, but it is reproducible, not random in the run-to-run sense.  **H1 (RNG luck) is contradicted by the seed mechanism.**

## G. Verdict

### Hypothesis scoring

| hypothesis | verdict | note |
|---|---|---|
| H1 synthetic RNG luck | STRONG CONTRADICTION | Seed is `sum(ord(c) for c in ticker)` -- deterministic. Cannot be 'unlucky RNG.' |
| H2 real price tape unfavourable for AAPL | STRONG SUPPORT | AAPL full-window total return is strongly positive (+105.7%) but 9 of 13 stops cluster in a single Dec-2025 to Jan-2026 window where AAPL fell ~10%, a localised adverse drift not present elsewhere in the corpus. |
| H3 stop calibration vs vol regime | STRONG CONTRADICTION | AAPL median RV = 0.2363 (rank 3/10, +15% vs universe median).  But NVDA (highest vol, 0.3817) had only 1 stops, UNH (2nd, 0.3319) had 2, while AAPL (3rd) had 13.  Stop counts do NOT track vol rank. |
| H4 confidence model anti-predictive on AAPL | WEAK SUPPORT | Q5-Q1 10d directional-return spread: AAPL -0.0098 (n_q5=11, n_q1=6); ex-AAPL +0.0085 (n_q5=68, n_q1=63).  AAPL sample is too small for STRONG verdict either way. |
| H5 entry-condition mismatch | WEAK CONTRADICTION | All five entry-condition fields within 0.30 sigma of ex-AAPL means. |

### Most likely explanation

The AAPL stop concentration (-$4,697.99 across 13 stop-outs) is dominated by **H2 (real-price tape unfavourable for AAPL during a localised window)**.  9 of the 13 stops fall in a single Dec-2025 to Jan-2026 cluster during which AAPL fell ~10% (see Section B); the deterministic synthetic-sentiment generator happened to fire high-confidence BUY CALLS into that cluster, and a real adverse drift on AAPL during the hold periods tripped the stops as designed.  The remaining 4 stops are in a smaller Jan-2025 cluster on a similar adverse drift.

**H3 (vol-blind stop) is contradicted by the per-ticker data.**  H3 predicts that the highest-vol tickers should have the most stop-outs, since the 80% absolute-fraction stop ignores the underlying's vol regime.  But NVDA (highest median realised vol, 0.3817) had only 1 stops on 89 taken trades, UNH (2nd at 0.3319) had 2 on 6, while AAPL (3rd at 0.2363) had 13 on 44.  Stop counts do NOT track the vol ranking; if H3 were the dominant driver, NVDA should be the worst.  Vol-blind calibration may still be a latent issue, but it is not what's driving the AAPL anomaly here.

**H1 is contradicted by the deterministic seed mechanism.**  AAPL gets `seed = 286` on every run; the same synthetic sentiment path is generated every time, so this is not RNG luck.

**H4 has WEAK SUPPORT only.**  AAPL's Q5-Q1 directional return at 10d is mildly negative (-0.0098) vs ex-AAPL's mildly positive (+0.0085), consistent with the model carrying weaker predictive signal on AAPL than on other tickers.  But with n_q5=11 and n_q1=6 on AAPL specifically, the spread is well within sampling noise.  A real-data refit on the 10-ticker corpus could resolve this; the v2_10t fit is parked at `data/raw/confidence_model_v2_10t.json` for promotion when warranted.

**H5 is contradicted by the distributional evidence in Section C.**  AAPL's entry conditions on sentiment, confidence, iv_rank, momentum, and Kelly fraction are all within 0.30 sigma of the ex-AAPL universe.  Whatever's making AAPL underperform happens AFTER entry, not at entry.

**Confidence level: HIGH on H2 as the dominant driver.**  The cluster pattern is unambiguous in the data, and the underlying behaviour during those clusters explains the stop firings without requiring any production-code calibration issue.

### Recommendation

**Phase 2 (fix) is NOT triggered.**  Per the prompt's gating rule: 'H1 or H2 conclusive -> no fix is appropriate.  Document the finding and move on.'  H2 (real-price tape unfavourable for AAPL during a localised Dec-2025/Jan-2026 cluster) is the dominant cause, with STRONG SUPPORT.  H3, H4, H5 do not carry STRONG SUPPORT.

**Action: documentation only.**  The stop-loss mechanic did its job (capped each loser at ~80% of debit instead of letting them expire worthless).  The drag is real but it's a property of how the synthetic-sentiment generator's deterministic AAPL path interacted with a real ~10% AAPL drawdown window.  No production-code change is justified by this evidence.

**Future work to resolve remaining ambiguity:** rerun diagnosis on a real-mode corpus (`scripts/r3_baseline.py --real`).  H4 (WEAK SUPPORT here) becomes more diagnosable on a larger AAPL sample with non-synthetic sentiment.  If AAPL's stop concentration reproduces on real data, the v2_10t logistic refit (`data/raw/confidence_model_v2_10t.json`) is the next lever to test.
